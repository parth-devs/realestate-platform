PERSONA 04: INDEPENDENT LANDLORD

David & Sarah Chen — Small-Scale Residential Investors (1–10 Units)

LANDLORD TIER — FREE LISTINGS & RENT RAILS
PROFILE & CONTEXT

Self-managing owners of residential rental properties in Texas suburban hubs (e.g., Plano, Pearland, New Braunfels). Seek professional vacancy marketing and compliant tenant screening without hiring property managers.

CORE OPERATIONAL GOALS
  • Publish verified rental listings with automated syndication to major consumer search channels.
  • Review complete applicant screening packages (credit score, criminal background, eviction record).
  • Draft and execute Texas-specific residential lease agreements with compliant digital signatures.
  • Collect monthly rent via automated ACH direct deposit with automated late fee calculation.
CRITICAL PAIN POINTS & TEXAS RISKS

Tenant defaults and non-payment; non-compliance with Texas Property Code Chapter 92 (strict 30-day security deposit accounting and statutory repair response deadlines).

SYSTEM PERMISSIONS & ACCESS
  • Read/Write access to owned property listings, tenant applications, credit reports, lease agreements, and payment ledgers.
  • Administrative ability to invite tenants, adjust rent schedules, and log manual maintenance entries.
PERSONA 05: REAL ESTATE AGENT & LAND BROKER

Javier Morales — Residential & Acreage Specialist

PROFESSIONAL / AGENT TIER — PAID PROMOTIONS
PROFILE & CONTEXT

Licensed Texas broker or sales agent representing residential buyers, home sellers, and rural acreage clients. Operates between suburban subdivisions and rural ranches.

CORE OPERATIONAL GOALS
  • Promote active MLS listings with high-visibility badges and agent contact cards.
  • Verify boundary lines, ownership history, and CAD assessments during client field tours.
  • Receive qualified buyer inquiries and tour scheduling requests directly into a dedicated inbox.
  • Share curated property collections with clients, complete with private agent notes.
CRITICAL PAIN POINTS & TEXAS RISKS

Unqualified leads; outdated county tax data during client negotiations; compliance with Texas Real Estate Commission (TREC) mandatory consumer notice postings (IABS and Consumer Protection Notice).

SYSTEM PERMISSIONS & ACCESS
  • Read/Write access to professional profile, claimed listings, lead management inbox, and client collaboration folders.
  • Enhanced cadastral mapping tools with high-resolution aerial and topographical layers.
