Build vs. Buy / Outside Service Provider Matrix

To maximize capital efficiency and time-to-market, the platform strictly delineates between core proprietary capabilities built internally and commoditized external datasets or regulated financial services licensed from specialized service provider categories.

Platform Capability Strategy Service Provider Category Operational Rationale & Integration
Cadastral Vector Mapping Tiles Licensed Partner Commercial Cadastral Parcel Aggregator & State GIS Bureau (TxGIO) Avoids negotiating 254 individual county parcel licenses; leverages pre-standardized geometry while syncing directly with top Texas metro CADs.
