TEXAS PROPERTY SEARCH & MANAGEMENT PLATFORM SECTION 5: FEATURE INVENTORY (MAP & SEARCH ENGINES)
SECTION 05

Comprehensive Feature Inventory (Areas 1 & 2)

The platform features nine core capability suites designed for seamless consumer discovery, land inspection, and property management. Each capability is defined below in functional, non-technical business terms.

AREA 01

Interactive Cadastral Map & Parcel Boundary Engine

Delivers fluid, statewide spatial visualization uniting cadastral land boundary vectors, satellite imagery, topographical data, and active real estate marketplace listings across Texas.

Capability Functional Description & Operational Behavior Texas Operational Relevance
Statewide Vector Parcel Overlay High-performance rendering of discrete property boundary polygons across all 254 Texas counties. Parcels highlight on tap or hover with gold boundary strokes. Standardizes fragmented county parcel boundaries into a single continuous statewide map.
Multi-Layer Base Map Switching Allows users to toggle instantly between: High-Resolution Aerial/Satellite Imagery, Clean Street Map, USGS Topographic Contours, and Terrain Hybrid. Vital for evaluating rural terrain, elevation contours, tree cover, water features, and access roads across Texas ranches.
Real-Time Mobile GPS Tracking Integrates device GPS to position user with a pulsating location indicator and directional compass orientation relative to legal parcel lines. Enables field agents and acreage buyers to walk fence lines and identify parcel corners directly on-site in remote areas.
Interactive Polygon Search Enables users to draw freehand lasso or polygon boundaries around specific neighborhoods, school attendance zones, or rural corridors. Allows buyers to isolate specific subdivision sections or rural school districts, bypassing rigid city limits.
Parcel Measurement Suite On-screen geometric measurement tools calculating linear perimeter distance (feet/miles) and enclosed polygon surface area (square feet/acres). Critical for measuring road frontage, fence requirements, and prospective building footprints on Texas acreage.
Offline Map Caching Allows users to pre-download parcel geometries and aerial base maps for selected counties or bounding boxes prior to field travel. Ensures uninterrupted navigation across rural West, South, and Central Texas counties with spotty cellular coverage.
AREA 02

Search, Multi-Attribute Filtering & Discovery Engine

A flexible omni-channel search framework enabling rapid discovery of residential sales, rental vacancies, rural acreage, and unlisted cadastral parcels.

Capability Functional Description & Operational Behavior Texas Operational Relevance
Omni-Search Bar Unified search field with intelligent auto-complete parsing: Street Addresses, City/Metro names, Zip Codes, County + APN/Parcel ID, Owner Surnames, and MLS IDs. Supports both consumer home shopping (e.g., "Austin, TX") and professional parcel inquiry (e.g., "Harris County Parcel 104-582").
Standard Marketplace Filters Multi-criteria filtering: Price range, monthly rent range, bedrooms, bathrooms, home type (single-family, condo, townhome, multi-family, manufactured, land), and garage spaces. Reflects Texas housing stock variations from suburban master-planned developments to downtown high-rises.
Land & Acreage Filters Granular land criteria: Acreage range (e.g., 5–20 acres, 50–100 acres, 500+ acres), road access type, water well availability, perimeter fencing, and outbuildings. Allows rural buyers to isolate functional farm, ranch, and recreational parcels from standard residential lots.
Texas-Specific Tax & District Filters Filters by: Active 1-d-1 Agricultural Exemption status, Municipal Utility District (MUD) presence/absence, and Independent School District (ISD). Empowers buyers to filter out properties with high MUD tax burdens or locate parcels with qualified agricultural tax valuations.
