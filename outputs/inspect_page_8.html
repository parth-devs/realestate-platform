JOURNEY 03

Tenant Discovery, Reusable Screening Profile & Application Submission

Primary Actor: Marcus Vance (Prospective Renter)

Details the step-by-step path of a renter finding a vacancy, completing an FCRA-compliant background and credit check, and submitting a reusable application package to a Texas landlord.

1
Rental Discovery: Browses rental listings in Dallas-Fort Worth. Identifies a 3-bedroom single-family rental in Plano listed at $2,450/month with an "Apply with Portable Screening" badge.
2
Profile Assembly: Clicks "Apply Now". Enters personal identity info, employment history, declared monthly income ($8,500/mo), vehicle details, and uploads two recent paystubs and government photo ID.
3
Screening Authorization & Payment: Authorizes a soft-pull credit report, 50-state criminal record check, and nationwide eviction history. Pays one-time $35 platform fee providing 30 days of unlimited applications.
4
Verification Processing: Background verification service generates report within 2 minutes. Credit score (740), payment history, zero criminal records, and clean eviction history populated into private profile.
5
Application Submission: Reviews assembled application package. Clicks "Submit Application to Landlord". Adds an optional personal introduction note explaining move-in timeline and pet documentation.
6
Status Tracking & Notice: Dashboard status updates to "Under Landlord Review". System triggers notification when landlord opens the report. Displays expected decision timeline (typically 24–48 hours).
Expected Outcomes: Landlord receives instant notification of completed screening package; renter retains reusable report valid across all platform listings for 30 days.
Failure & Edge States: Credit bureau identity mismatch (prompts knowledge-based authentication questions); screening incomplete (blocks submission until mandatory fields verified).
JOURNEY 04

Landlord Listing Setup, Application Review, Lease Execution & Rent Rail

Primary Actor: David & Sarah Chen (Independent Landlords)

Walks through the landlord's complete lifecycle from vacancy marketing and applicant screening review to digital lease generation and automated ACH rent collection.

1
Listing Creation & Verification: Enters property address in Landlord Portal. System pulls CAD property records to verify ownership. Enters rent ($2,450), deposit ($2,450), pet rules, and uploads 15 photos. Listing syndicates live.
2
Application & Screening Review: Receives email alert: "New Application Received from Marcus Vance". Opens Landlord Dashboard. Reviews income verification, credit score (740), criminal, and eviction reports in structured view.
3
Applicant Acceptance: Clicks "Accept Applicant". System automatically updates application status and triggers an automated notification to applicant. System offers option to send adverse action notices to declined applicants.
4
Texas Digital Lease Creation: Launches digital lease builder. System auto-populates tenant info, property address, rent amount, and default Texas Property Code Chapter 92 statutory rules (smoke detectors, repair procedures, 30-day deposit return).
5
Electronic Signature Routing: Landlord reviews draft and clicks "Send for Signatures". Tenant receives secure e-sign link, reviews, and signs. Landlord countersigns digitally. System seals PDF and archives in both dashboards.
6
Automated ACH Rent Setup: Landlord links checking account via micro-deposit/instant verification. Tenant sets up recurring auto-pay on the 1st of each month. Move-in deposit and first month's rent schedule initiated with zero transaction fees.
Expected Outcomes: Vacancy filled, compliant Texas lease archived, recurring banking rail established, automatic late fees configured for grace period expiration on the 4th.
Failure & Edge States: Ownership name mismatch against CAD (requires manual deed upload); tenant fails to sign within 48 hours (triggers automated expiration warning).
