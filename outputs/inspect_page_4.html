TEXAS PROPERTY SEARCH & MANAGEMENT PLATFORM SECTION 2: RESEARCH METHODOLOGY & EVIDENCE LOG
SECTION 02

Research Methodology & Public Evidence Log

To design a competitive, robust platform, we conducted an exhaustive public review of two prevailing market archetypes: Archetype A (Cadastral Parcel & Field Intelligence Tool) and Archetype B (Residential Marketplace & Rental Management Suite). The investigation strictly utilized publicly accessible browser interfaces, published pricing schedules, user documentation, and visible operational workflows without circumventing access controls, paywalls, or private credentials.

CONFIRMED: Observed directly on public web/mobile screens, help docs, or published pricing. INFERRED: Operational deduction based on visible inputs, state transitions, and business logic. UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED: Private backend systems, closed algorithms, or undisclosed terms.
Functional Area Observed Public Behavior & Feature Mechanics Evidence Status Operational Translation for Texas Build
Cadastral Map & Parcels Vector polygon boundaries overlaid on satellite, street, and topo imagery. Tapping parcel reveals owner name, APN, acreage, and assessed tax values. CONFIRMED Build vector parcel tile pipeline integrating Texas Geographic Information Office (TxGIO) and county CAD files.
Live GPS Field Tracking Mobile GPS crosshairs locate user relative to property boundary lines in real time; drive mode centers map dynamically during transit. CONFIRMED Implement native browser/mobile geolocation with orientation compass for on-site Texas acreage and ranch evaluations.
Multi-User Corporate Tier Centralized admin dashboard with volume pricing tiers (10% off for 2–5 seats scaling to 50% for 101+ seats); seat invitation workflows. CONFIRMED Create self-service team management console with multi-seat volume billing and shared parcel folder workspaces.
Residential Listing Discovery Omni-search supporting city, zip, address, or custom-drawn polygon boundaries. Granular filters for beds, baths, price, home type, and amenities. CONFIRMED Integrate regional Texas MLS feeds with cadastral polygon boundaries to allow cross-filtering of acreage and residential specs.
Valuation Modeling Automated market value estimates and rental yield estimates with confidence bands and historical trend charts. CONFIRMED In Texas's non-disclosure environment, valuation estimates must derive from CAD records, active listings, and macroeconomic indicators.
Tenant Screening Workflow Applicant completes single reusable profile ($35 fee range) granting landlord access to soft-pull credit check, criminal history, and eviction record. CONFIRMED Integrate FCRA-compliant screening bureau; ensure soft inquiries do not depress applicant credit scores.
Digital Lease Execution Online lease builder using standardized state templates, custom rules, automated signature routing, and PDF storage. CONFIRMED Incorporate Texas-promulgated lease forms with Texas Property Code Chapter 92 statutory tenant and landlord disclosures.
Automated Rent Collection Zero-fee ACH bank transfers for landlords and tenants; convenience fee for card payments; recurring auto-pay and late fee automation. CONFIRMED Partner with banking-as-a-service provider to handle Automated Clearing House (ACH) transfers, tenant ledgering, and split payments.
Backend Fraud Moderation Automated algorithms flag suspicious listings, duplicate photos, and discriminatory phrasing; manual review queues for escalated disputes. INFERRED Deploy automated Fair Housing text scanners and CAD deed cross-referencing to prevent fraudulent listing creation.
Cadastral Data Latency County tax records reflect varying update cadences depending on local county appraisal district cycles (typically quarterly to annual). INFERRED Establish automated monthly ETL pipelines for large urban Texas CADs and quarterly/annual cycles for rural counties.
Proprietary Search Ranking Internal algorithms determine sort order for featured listings, agent advertising distribution, and search result relevance. UNKNOWN Establish an open, merit-based relevance algorithm based strictly on geographic proximity, listing completeness, and user filter match.
